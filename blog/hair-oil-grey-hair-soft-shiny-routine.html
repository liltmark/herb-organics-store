Hair Oil for Grey Hair: Keeping Grey Hair Soft and Shiny

Hair Care By Herb & Organics Co. 14 November 2026

Why grey hair feels different

Grey hair grows in when the hair loses its colour, and it often feels different from the hair around it. It can feel coarse, wiry, or dry, and it can be harder to comb into place. It also catches the light differently, so it can look dull or yellowish when it is dry, even though the hair itself is healthy.

The change is natural, and it does not mean the hair is damaged. It simply means the hair needs a little more moisture and a gentler routine than before. A light oiling routine suits grey hair well, because oil adds softness and shine without changing the colour or weighing the hair down.

What oil does for grey hair

Hair oil coats each strand with a thin, light layer that holds moisture in and keeps the hair soft to the touch. On grey hair, that softness matters most, since the hair can feel rough even after a good wash. A light coat of oil after washing, or a short oiling before the wash, makes the hair easier to comb and gives it a calm, even shine.

Oil also helps with the frizz that grey hair often shows, since dry strands lift and separate more easily. A small amount smoothed over the surface of the hair keeps the strands lying together, and the hair looks neater all day. Our guide to a light leave-in for flyaways and frizz uses the same idea, with a little oil on the surface rather than a heavy coat.

The oiling routine, step by step

Start with dry or slightly damp hair, and warm a small amount of Nabat hair oil between the palms. Work it through the mid-lengths and ends first, using slow, downward strokes, and then smooth what is left on the palms over the top layer of the hair. Keep the amount small: a coin-sized amount is enough for most hair, and more oil does not mean more shine.

If the scalp feels dry, a little oil massaged into the scalp with the fingertips helps too, but keep it light and wash it out within a few hours. Our scalp massage technique with hair oil explains the slow, light movements that work best, and a short massage once or twice a week is enough.

Washing grey hair gently

Grey hair needs a gentle wash, since a harsh wash strips the moisture the oil has just added. Wash with lukewarm water and a small amount of herbal shampoo, working the lather into the scalp with the fingertips and letting it run down the lengths. Our gentle routine for grey hair with herbal shampoo follows the same calm order, and it pairs well with the oiling routine here.

Rinse well and pat the hair dry with a soft towel, instead of rubbing, since grey hair can be more fragile when it is wet. A second wash is rarely needed, and the hair should feel soft and clean, not squeaky or stripped.

Keeping grey hair shiny day to day

Shine on grey hair comes from moisture and smooth strands, not from heavy products. A tiny amount of oil smoothed over the surface of dry hair in the morning keeps the strands lying flat and catches the light evenly. If the hair looks dull by the afternoon, a light smoothing with damp hands is enough to refresh it.

The sun can make grey hair look yellowish over time, so a little care in strong sun helps. Our guide to hair oil for sun protection shows how a light coat before going out keeps the hair soft in strong sun, and the same light coat works for grey hair. Remember that oil is not a sunscreen, and it does not replace shade or a hat in strong sun.

How often to oil grey hair

Once or twice a week is enough for most grey hair, as a short oiling before the wash or a light coat after it. Very dry or coarse grey hair can take a little more, with a light coat on the ends every other day, but the amount should always stay small.

The hair will tell you if the routine is right. If it feels soft, combs easily, and looks shiny, the amount is right. If it looks flat or feels heavy, use less oil next time. Our guide to hair oil in the wedding season shows how a light shine routine works for special days, and the same small amount suits everyday grey hair too.

Mistakes to avoid

The first mistake is using too much oil, which weighs grey hair down and makes it look flat and greasy. The second is oiling the roots heavily every day, which leaves the scalp oily and the hair hard to wash clean. The third is washing grey hair with very hot water, which strips the moisture the oil has added.

The fourth mistake is brushing grey hair hard when it is dry, which breaks the strands and adds frizz. And the fifth is expecting oil to change the colour of grey hair, which it will not do: oil keeps grey hair soft and shiny, and that is enough. Oil lightly, wash gently, and grey hair will stay soft, neat, and bright.
