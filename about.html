About Herb & Organics Co.

Handcrafted Organics | Pure & Natural Organic Ingredients | Skin-Loving Formulas

Herb & Organics Co. is a family-run brand from Pakistan. Everything we make starts with one simple belief: hair and skin care should come from pure, natural organic ingredients — not from harsh chemicals.

Our products are handcrafted with care by our family. When you message us, you are talking directly to the people behind the brand — we take your order personally, confirm every detail on WhatsApp, and send your parcel with Cash on Delivery, anywhere in Pakistan.

What We Make

Our first love is Beldi Black Soap — the traditional Moroccan olive-based paste soap, treasured for generations as the secret to soft, clean and glowing skin. It deep cleanses, gently exfoliates and unclogs pores, and it is the heart of the hammam ritual.

Then came NABAT Herbal & Organic Shampoo — a gentle, plant-based shampoo built around Amla and Reetha, with 50+ natural ingredients. It is sulphate free and paraben free, and it nourishes hair from root to tip. A third member of the family, NABAT Hair Oil, is coming soon.
